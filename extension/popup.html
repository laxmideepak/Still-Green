Please read what the extension does with your Slack login. You see this again if you change the Stillgreen address.

You changed the Stillgreen address.

What it reads
Your Slack session token and the “d” cookie, for the Slack workspaces open in Chrome.
What they allow
Anyone who has them can act as you in Slack, like being signed in.
Where they go
Only to the Stillgreen address you set (127.0.0.1:8765). Never to the website or anyone else. This address is not this computer, so your Slack login will be kept on that server. Only use a server you control.
Where they are kept
On your computer or your server, in a file only your user can read.
How to remove them
Click Remove in Stillgreen. Then in Slack go to Settings and click “Sign out of all other sessions”.

Slack does not officially support keeping you active, and your employer may not allow it. Slack decides your final status.
